# Oportunidad de la semana

**Goal:** point builders at one real, open opportunity (a hackathon, bounty, grant or open call)
and get them to form or find a team in the Telegram group.

**Material:** one verified opportunity. Open its public page and confirm it is still open on the
week's Monday. Put the link and the deadline in `plan.md`. **No verified opportunity, no video
that week.** Never invent, guess or recycle a closed one. Don't build around Solana Foundation
programmes.

**Every week:** at most one.

## Structure (40–55 s, 9:16)

| Beat | Time | Scene | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–3 s | stat reveal | the prize, the deadline or the number of days left |
| What | 3–15 s | title card | name of the opportunity and who runs it |
| Who | 15–28 s | comparison | who it is for, and what you need to apply |
| How | 28–42 s | timeline | the key dates |
| CTA | 42–55 s | recap card | "Arma tu equipo en el Telegram" + `t.me/solana_colombia` |

## Narration shape

The number that matters → what it is → who should apply → the dates → "find a team in the
group". State the deadline as a date, never "soon".

## Post text

- **YouTube title:** `<Opportunity>: <prize or deadline>`, ≤ 70 characters.
- **Body, all platforms:** what it is, the deadline, the official link, then
  `Busca equipo en el Telegram: t.me/solana_colombia` and `Voz generada con IA.`

## Queue row

Goes into `weekly_queue` as `kind = 'oportunidad'`: `title` is the name and deadline, `body` one
or two lines, `link` the official page.

## Keep it varied

Lead with a different number each week (prize, days left, team size) and alternate the scene
order of "Who" and "How".
