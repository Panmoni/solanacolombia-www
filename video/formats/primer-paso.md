# Primer paso (mindset)

**Goal:** one concrete mindset move for a builder who knows enough but hasn't shipped: posting
work in public, finishing small, shipping before feeling ready, asking a precise question.
Mindset is half the work; this format is about the half that isn't technical.

**Material:** one real, citable example that shows the move: a known project's first version, a
public changelog, a challenge result posted in the group (with consent), a quote with its source.
Link it in `plan.md`. No generic motivation without an example.

**Every week:** at most one.

## Structure (40–55 s, 9:16)

| Beat | Time | Scene | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–3 s | title card | the uncomfortable truth, one line ("Nadie va a ver tu código si no lo publicas") |
| Example | 3–20 s | before/after split or timeline | the real example |
| Move | 20–38 s | recap card | the one move, stated as an action |
| Today | 38–48 s | UI reveal | the smallest version of it you can do today |
| CTA | 48–55 s | recap card | "Hazlo hoy y cuéntalo en el Telegram" + `t.me/solana_colombia` |

## Narration shape

The truth → the example → the move → the smallest version you can do today → the group. Speak
like a peer, not a coach. No "you can do it", no hustle talk.

## Post text

- **YouTube title:** the move, as an imperative, ≤ 70 characters.
- **Body, all platforms:** the move, the example's link, then
  `Cuéntalo en el Telegram: t.me/solana_colombia` and `Voz generada con IA.`

## Keep it varied

Never repeat a move within eight weeks. Alternate the example scene (split one week, timeline the
next).
