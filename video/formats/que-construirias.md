# ¿Qué construirías?

**Goal:** a question video. Put one product idea next to a real Colombian problem and ask the
viewer how they would build it. The answers happen in the Telegram group, which is the point.

**Material:** one unused idea from `src/content/blog/200-ideas.md` (name its number in
`plan.md`) and one real Colombian context it fits (Nequi or Daviplata habits, remittances,
Rappi couriers, informal commerce). Keep it factual; cite a source for any statistic.

**Every week:** at most one.

## Structure (35–50 s, 9:16)

| Beat | Time | Scene | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–3 s | title card | the problem as a question ("¿Y si al domiciliario le pagaran cada minuto?") |
| Problem | 3–15 s | stat reveal | the Colombian context, with a source |
| Idea | 15–30 s | flow diagram | the idea in three nodes |
| Question | 30–42 s | recap card | the open question: stack, first feature, or who pays |
| CTA | 42–50 s | recap card | "Respóndelo en el Telegram" + `t.me/solana_colombia` |

## Narration shape

The problem as a question → the context → the idea in plain words → the open question, asked
twice → the group. End on the question, not an answer.

## Post text

- **YouTube title:** the question, ≤ 70 characters.
- **Body, all platforms:** the question, the idea's number in the "200 ideas" list with the blog
  link, then `Respóndelo en el Telegram: t.me/solana_colombia` and `Voz generada con IA.`

## Keep it varied

Rotate the open question's angle (stack, first feature, business model) and never use the same
Colombian context two weeks running.
